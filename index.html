FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I really get enough protein without paneer or whey?

Yes. With dals, legumes, soya, tofu, sattu and peanuts, most people can reach their protein needs. Your exact requirement depends on age, weight, activity and health.

Will this plan help me lose weight?

The plan is high in protein and fibre, which keeps you full. Weight loss depends on total calories, so adjust rice, roti, oil and nuts to your goal.

I don't like tofu. Can I still follow it?

Yes. Replace tofu with soya chunks, extra dal, chana, rajma, sprouts or besan dishes — the protein totals stay similar.
